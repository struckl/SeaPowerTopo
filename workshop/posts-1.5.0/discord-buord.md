## Topographic Map 1.5.0 – new buttons, 9 new looks
Land heights and sea depths on the tactical map – 1.5.0 has new buttons and 9 new looks:
- **Title bar**: one small icon group – mountain (Topography), the look's colours (list of looks), Legend, crosshair (cursor height)
- **9 new looks**: Soviet General Staff map, Bathymetric chart, Desert storm, Satellite, 1940s chart, Recon photo, Survey sonar, White phosphor, Amber display
- **8 looks removed** (Atlas, Paper sea chart, Parchment, Quiet, Atlas dusk, ECDIS dusk, Thermal imager, Neon); 19 in all

**Which game version?** One item, a 0.8.7 build and a globe build; the right one switches on by itself. 1.5.0 was run in the game on 0.8.7.
**Beta heads-up**: Anchor Chain doesn't start on the beta yet (issue #19 (<https://github.com/seapower-modders/AnchorChain/issues/19>)), so no AC mod loads there until it's updated.
